# Market queue

Firms we have verified and have not published, and firms we could not verify, with the reason
for each. It exists because a discovery run finds more firms than a publishing pass can measure,
and the ones left over are worth keeping rather than rediscovering: a firm with 2,652 reviews
that we could not read is a fact about our crawler, not about the firm, and the next person to
open this market should start from what is already known.

Review counts are summed across a firm's verified Google listings on the date given. They move.

## Buffalo, family law

The second family law market, opened 2026-09-18 with 24 firms. A Places run across Buffalo and
eight surrounding towns for "divorce lawyer Buffalo NY" returned 151 firms, 142 of them not
already published here. Every one of those 142 had its own home page read, and 32 present this
practice on it, which is the rule the New York City family law cohort set out. The market rule is
the one the Buffalo personal injury cohort already uses: an office in Erie or Niagara county,
meaning a postal code from 14000 to 14399, with Rochester at 144xx and above being a different
market.

What this market looks like is worth saying plainly, because it is not New York City. The largest
family practice here holds 407 reviews and the second holds 80. Nothing else reaches fifty. Above
those two, a divorce search returns a criminal defence firm with 554 reviews, another with 490, a
third with 413, and then elder law, traffic tickets, eviction and personal injury. Applying the
same rule to a market this thin admits general practices whose reviews are not all family law
reviews, which is a known cost of the rule and the reason it is written down rather than assumed:
in New York City it cut twenty general practices out of a ranking, and here it is most of what
keeps one honest.

### What their websites are like

Of the 142, 120 could be read and 22 could not, and the reasons are mostly a finding about this
market rather than about our crawler.

Twelve fail before HTTP has anything to say. Four firms publish over plain HTTP with no HTTPS on
either host: miskellandmoxham.com, falkandfalk.com, jamesprenda.com and joefrazierlaw.com. Three
domains no longer resolve: greenelawpllc.com, legalservicesbuffalony.com and mdvlaw.co. Three
refuse the connection outright: roscettidecastro.com, jonwilsonlaw.com and cotterandcotterpc.com.
nbrlaw.com is parked for sale by a domain broker, and lorigo.com returns 404 on its own home page.

Of the other ten, four return HTTP 403, which is bot protection and not something we work around:
dukelawfirm.net, gordondivorcelawfirm.com, cleedlaw.com and orchardparkny.org. Two rate-limit us
at 429, tupchiklegal.com and todoroinjurylaw.com, and are worth a slower retry. Four answer
normally and serve a page with no title element at all: divorcewny.com, muscatoshatkin.com,
berzerwolf.com and tillsvisalaw.com.

Four more were readable only after the crawler learned to try the www host, and one of them, Feron
Poleon LLP, is in the published cohort.
